AIRCRAFT · 50 KM

Visible flights are auto-enriched when possible. Verified times require a configured flight-status API and are never inferred from ADS-B.
Select an aircraft marker or list item to view its flight number, route, destination, aircraft type, speed, heading and detailed departure/arrival times.

Changi activity mode shows confirmed ground aircraft and nearby low-altitude flights separately; airborne aircraft are never labeled taxiing. Changi ground mode shows only reported aircraft on the ground within approximately 8.5 km of WSSS, including stopped and moving aircraft. It includes all airlines, but ADS-B ground reception is incomplete and this is not a certified airport-movement display. International coastlines use GSHHG shoreline geography; Singapore's highlighted main-island outline uses GSHHG geographic shoreline data rather than the previous hand-traced decoration. Street-map tiles, when selected, are © OpenStreetMap contributors. Runway strips for Singapore use published CAAS coordinates; international runway ends, when available, use OurAirports public-domain data. Runway 02R/20L at Changi is not available for civil use until at least 24 Dec 2026 under the published notice. The display cannot reliably tell whether a stopped aircraft is parked, holding short or waiting for a departure clearance. Climbing and descending aircraft near airports are labelled possible departures/arrivals only, because ADS-B does not prove which airport or runway they are using. Nearby flight cards automatically request detailed departure and arrival times when a flight-status source is configured, and those times are shown in Singapore time (SGT); otherwise they remain unavailable. Flight times may have provider usage charges. Singapore airport overlay shows Changi (WSSS), Seletar (WSSL) and Paya Lebar (WSAP); it is not a verified airport surface movement chart. Ground positions may be absent, and reported aircraft are NOT guaranteed to include all aircraft at the airport. Range controls: + / − buttons, mouse wheel, or two-finger pinch. The 100 km data feed is filtered to your selected viewing range. Estimated time to the selected airport assumes a straight line at current groundspeed and is not a verified landing time. Airport pairs are looked up separately using ADSBdb and may reflect a published route rather than the actual itinerary today; missing routes remain unavailable. Aircraft positions are live ADS-B data from adsb.lol contributors (ODbL 1.0). This is a geographic visualization, not an official aeronautical chart; coastline and airport data may be simplified or outdated. Aircraft markers animate between ADS-B reports using reported groundspeed and track; their short animated trails between updates are estimates, not verified historical positions. Reception may be incomplete or delayed. This is not an official ATC radar and must not be used for flight operations.
